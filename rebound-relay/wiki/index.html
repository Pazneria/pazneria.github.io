How to play

Control your bumper and knock the ball into the green target. Score as many points as you can before the timer ends.

01

Start a run

  1. 1

    Select a mode

    Click or tap Chill, Relay, or Overdrive, then select Start. Chill has the widest targets and slowest ball.

  2. 2

    Steer into the ball

    The ball launches automatically. Use WASD, the arrow keys, or the touch joystick to steer your bumper into it. Release steering to brake.

  3. 3

    Aim for the target

    The whole ball must fit within the green target when it reaches the wall. A miss rebounds off the wall, so keep playing.

  4. 4

    Follow the next target

    A goal adds points and moves the target clockwise. The ball rebounds without resetting either the ball or your bumper to the center.

There are no breaks between goals. The ball keeps moving at a minimum speed, and the run ends when the timer reaches zero.

02

Goal value and wall hits

Each new target starts worth 500 points. Each unique wall the ball hits before a goal deducts 100 from that goal's value. Repeated hits on the same wall make no further deduction, and your bumper touching a wall does not count.

When a goal scores

A bounce outside the green target counts as a wall hit, even on the target's wall. Hitting the target awards the remaining value without another deduction. The next target resets to 500, and all four walls can count again.

Bumping the ball does not clear its counted walls. Wall hits affect the next goal's value; they never subtract points you have already scored.

Repeated-wall example

North hit → North hit → West hit → Target

This goal scores 300 points: North and West each cost 100, while the second North hit costs nothing.

SHOT VALUE shows what the next goal will score. The N/E/S/W indicators show which walls have already counted for that target.

Scoring formula and minimum value

points = 500 - 100 × unique walls hit

With only four walls to count, the lowest goal value is 100. Repeated bounces cannot bring it to zero. A 0.2-second lock after a goal prevents immediate duplicate scoring; the just-scored wall does not count during that lock.

03

Target order and placement

Where the target moves

Every run starts with a target on North, the top wall. Goals advance the target clockwise: North → East → South → West → North.

The position along each wall is random, including the first target. It stays fixed until you score. Look for the green target in the arena to line up your next bump.

How the ball fits

Aim inside the opening rather than at its edge. The ball's center must be at least its 13-pixel radius inside either end of the target for the whole ball to fit.

Target spacing

Distances use a 960 × 640-pixel arena that scales to your screen. Target ends stay at least 36 pixels from a corner. After a goal, the next target's nearest edge is at least 240 pixels from the ball's center at the scoring contact.

04

Choose a difficulty

Click or tap Chill, Relay, or Overdrive before starting or after a run finishes. With a keyboard, press Tab to focus a mode button, then Space or Enter to select it. The chosen button says Selected; the others say Select.

Choosing a mode prepares the run. Press Tab to focus Start, then Enter to begin; the playfield receives keyboard focus automatically. Difficulty is locked during a run, including while paused. All three modes use the same timer and scoring rule, with a separate best for each mode.

05

Keyboard and touch controls

Keyboard

Tab, then Space / Enter
Focus and select a mode button before starting or after a run finishes.
WASD / arrow keys
Steer your bumper. Release to brake.
Space
Boost in your steering direction, or your last steering direction if you are not steering.
Enter
Start, resume a paused run, or play again after the result.
Esc
Pause or resume an active run.
R
Restart immediately in the selected mode.

When a button has keyboard focus, Enter or Space activates that button. Starting or resuming focuses the playfield automatically. To return focus after using another button, press Tab until the playfield is focused, or click or tap it.

Touch

Drag the on-screen joystick with your left thumb to steer; release it to brake. Tap Boost with your right thumb while continuing to steer.

Tap Pause / Resume to stop or continue the run, or Restart to begin again. Releasing or cancelling a joystick touch clears its steering input.

BOOST
Boost recharges in 1 second.

Press or tap again when it is ready. Holding Space or Boost does not repeat it, and a press during recharge will not fire later. The display shows BOOST READY or the remaining recharge time.

Movement speeds and boost

The steering speed limit is 340 arena pixels per second. Boost adds a 260-pixel-per-second impulse in your steering direction and raises the speed limit to 560 for 0.28 seconds. Bumping transfers momentum to the ball; boost does not aim it for you.

06

Settings and local bests

Ⅱ

Pause and restart

Pause stops the timer and keeps your positions, score and counted walls. Switching tabs or windows also pauses the game and clears steering input. Select Resume when you return.

Restart begins a fresh run in the same mode, resetting the score, timer, counted walls and boost recharge. It discards the unfinished run, which cannot update your best.

The game's Guide link opens a new tab. Switch back to the original game tab to resume that run; Back to game opens a fresh game page.

♪

Sound

Select Sound to turn effects on or off. Sound begins after a play gesture such as Start or Restart, or after turning it on. Muting stops queued tones, and the setting is saved when browser storage is available.

≈

Calm

Calm: on removes camera shake, particles, expanding rings and trails. Goal points remain visible without rising, and the physics, scoring and difficulty stay the same.

Calm follows your system's reduced-motion setting by default. An explicit choice is saved when browser storage is available.

★

Local bests

Only completed runs can update your best. Each mode saves its own record in this browser; records do not sync to another browser or device. Restarting keeps existing bests.

If storage is blocked or fails, Tab best lasts only until this game page is closed or reloaded. Earlier bonus-scoring bests remain separate and are not compared with the current records.

07

Current limits

Local play

The game needs no account or online scoring service and can open directly from index.html. At sustained frame rates below 10 FPS, the timer can run slower than real time.

Planned features

Leaderboards and obstacle maps are planned but unavailable. This version has no online score submission or map selector.

Physical-phone controls, audible sound quality and difficulty balance still need playtesting.

Version and source

This guide covers Playtest 0.4.1, checked on October 1, 2026. Source versions and SHA-256 hashes identify the files used to verify the rules.
